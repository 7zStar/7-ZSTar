notes:    version macros verified in fast-lzma2.h (FL2_VERSION_MAJOR/MINOR/
          RELEASE = 1.0.1). Fast LZMA2 has no independent .7z coder id or
          standalone archive format of its own: it registers as an
          *alternative encoder* for the official LZMA2 coder id (0x21, see
          7zHeader.h k_LZMA2), producing a standard LZMA2 bitstream that the
          official (unmodified) NCompress::NLzma2::CDecoder can decode --
          only the encoder differs (multi-threaded radix-match-finder
          instead of the official single-threaded LzFind). See
          CPP/7zip/Compress/FastLzma2Encoder.h/.cpp (new files -- this
          multi-threaded encoder is intentionally kept out of the official
          Lzma2Encoder.h/.cpp to honor this project's minimal-official-diff
          constraint) and FastLzma2Register.cpp.
